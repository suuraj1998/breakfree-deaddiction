Privacy Policy for BreakFree

Last updated: October 2026

BreakFree: De-Addiction & Sobriety Platform is committed to protecting your privacy and personal recovery journey. We operate on a strict privacy-by-design architecture where sensitive health logs and sobriety counters stay securely on your device.

1. Information We Collect and Process

BreakFree does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Here is what is processed:

  • Sobriety Milestones & Logs: Relapse journals, urges, streak milestones, and emergency guardian contacts are stored locally in your browser/device storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage).
  • Location (Geofencing Alerts): Used strictly on-device to warn you when entering user-configured trigger zones (bars, smoke shops). Location coordinates are never transmitted or stored on external servers.
  • Audio & Alarm System: The Sentinel alarm system synthesizes high-frequency audio tones locally. We do NOT record your ambient audio or eavesdrop through your microphone.
  • AI Neural Counselor: Queries submitted to the AI recovery assistant are processed to generate instant coping mechanisms. Never submit identifiable medical records or government IDs.

2. Device Permissions Required

  • Location (GPS): Used solely to provide real-time distance proximity alerts for user-defined danger zones.
  • Notifications: To deliver scheduled streak milestones, recovery check-ins, and guardian escalation alerts.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Links

BreakFree may integrate non-intrusive display banners and sponsored wellness aids (such as cold showers, breathwork tools, and journals). We adhere strictly to Google Play Store ad policies and family-safe advertising standards.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You maintain 100% control over your data. You can clear all sobriety logs, streak history, and emergency settings at any time by clearing your app data or clicking "Factory Reset App" within the Admin Settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Google Play compliance, please reach out to our team at: support@breakfree-recovery.com.
